`factory.reabank` is the factory bank file of Reaticulate
(https://github.com/jtackaberry/reaticulate), by Jason Tackaberry,
under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE.md` beside it, Reaticulate's own,
which also says its notation icons are not under that licence: none are
here). It is kept as it is, unchanged, as a fixture: the tests read every
bank of it into articulation maps.
